# Code of conduct

Be kind and be specific. Assume good faith, criticise code and ideas rather than people, and keep discussion on the technical point. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination are not welcome here.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments and may block contributors who repeatedly break these rules. To report a problem privately, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or contact the maintainer, Mikey Nichols, through the email on his GitHub profile.
